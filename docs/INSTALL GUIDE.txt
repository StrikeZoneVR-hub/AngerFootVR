ANGER FOOT VR - INSTALL GUIDE
Mod by StrikeZoneVR. Free, unofficial and fan-made: https://github.com/StrikeZoneVR-hub/AngerFootVR

WHAT YOU NEED
- Anger Foot on Steam (PC).
- A PC VR headset: Quest 2/3/Pro through Virtual Desktop, Air Link/Link or Steam Link, or a SteamVR headset
  (Index, Vive, Windows Mixed Reality).
- An internet connection the first time (the installer downloads Unity's VR plugin from Unity).

INSTALL (about a minute)
1. Find your game folder: in Steam, right-click Anger Foot > Manage > Browse local files.
2. Unzip this zip INTO that folder, so "Anger Foot VR.exe" sits next to "Anger Foot.exe".
3. Run "Anger Foot VR.exe" (the mod's launcher).
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway" (the launcher isn't signed).
4. Start your VR app first (Virtual Desktop, the Meta/Oculus app or SteamVR), then press PLAY IN VR.
   The first time, it installs the mod (BepInEx plus the mod) before starting the game.
   - If your antivirus complains about winhttp.dll: that's BepInEx's mod loader; allow it.
5. Put the headset on: you start in the movie house.

If the game starts on the wrong VR app, open OPTIONS > VR RUNTIME in the launcher and pick yours.
The VR CHECK page shows what the mod sees.

CONTROLS (Quest/Touch; Index, Vive and WMR are mapped too)
Left hand:
  Stick ............ move
  Hold stick in .... recenter
  X ................ kick
  Y ................ shoe power (in the car: your shoes)
  Grip ............. pick up / use what your hand reaches or points at (bottles, drinks, doors),
                     or hold the front of a two-handed gun
  Menu ............. pause
Right hand:
  Stick ............ turn (snap or smooth)
  Click stick ...... the middle finger
  B ................ kick
  A ................ jump
  Trigger .......... shoot
  Grip ............. hold it, then let go: throw your gun (swing to throw it hard)
The gun hand can be switched to the left in the settings.

GOOD TO KNOW
- The menus play on a big screen in the movie house: point at it and pull the trigger.
  Lists scroll with the right stick while you point at them, or hold the trigger and drag.
- The game's SETTINGS has a VR tab (the headset icon) with every VR option.
- The level select is in the car: point at a level on the map book, pull the trigger to pick it, again to play.
- Kicking: B or X. Jump and kick while moving for a flying double kick.
- Your saves are never touched by the mod.

UNINSTALL
Run "Anger Foot VR.exe", go to VR CHECK, press UNINSTALL (twice: it asks to be sure). The game folder goes back
to how it was.

FOUND A BUG?
Open an issue on the GitHub page (or tell StrikeZoneVR) with what happened and the log: in the launcher, VR CHECK > GAME LOG
(it's the file BepInEx\LogOutput.log in the game folder).
